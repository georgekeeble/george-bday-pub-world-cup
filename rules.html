🏆 George's Pub World Cup Rules

Teams

🇧🇷 Brazil

🏴 England

🇩🇪 Germany

🇦🇷 Argentina

Pub 1 - Team Reveal

  • Teams announced
  • Captains appointed
  • Fixtures released
  • No competition

Pub 2 - Group Stage

Each team member must find a drink beginning with the same letter as their first name.

Examples:

  • George → Guinness
  • Tom → Thatchers
  • Amber → Aperol Spritz

Each valid drink equals 1 goal.

Highest score wins the match.

🍛 Curry Transfer Window

  • Transfer window opens
  • Players may be bought, sold or loaned
  • Transfer announcements appear on website
  • Starting budget: £100m

Pub 3 - Semi Finals

Konpira Fune Fune

  • 1st vs 4th
  • 2nd vs 3rd
  • Best of 3 rounds

Pub 4 - Finals

Pint Chop

  • Third Place Playoff
  • World Cup Final
  • Best of 3 rounds

🍺 Beer Economy

Every complete team round purchased earns:

+1 League Point

Each full round also costs:

£20m from your transfer budget

🎥 VAR

Each team receives 2 VAR challenges.

If VAR is called:

The challenge is replayed.

No discussions. No voting. No referee decisions.

National Abilities

🇧🇷 Brazil - Brazilian Flair

Once per tournament.

If successful, a challenge is worth 2 goals.

If unsuccessful, it is worth 0 goals.

🏴 England - Bellingham Time

Once per tournament.

After losing a challenge, England may invoke:

"What would Jude do?"

The challenge enters sudden death.

🇩🇪 Germany - German Efficiency

Once per tournament.

Replay a failed challenge immediately.

🇦🇷 Argentina - Dark Arts

Once per tournament.

Force the opposition's best performer to repeat a challenge.
