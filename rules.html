📜 George's Birthday Pub World Cup Rules

🏆 Objective

The team with the highest total points at the end of the tournament wins the World Cup (and more importantly will recieve a brown envelope of £75 from Mr Infantino).

⚽ Match Points

  • Win = 3 Points
  • Draw = 1 Point
  • Loss = 0 Points

🍺 Beer Bonus Points

A team receives 1 bonus league point each time a full team round is bought.

🚨 Transfer Window

The transfer window opens at the curry stop.

Teams are free to negotiate transfers, all transfers must be swap deals with other teams. Eg. Brazil loans Fred to Argentina for one round in exchange for MacAllister and a round of crisps.

All transfers must be agreed by both teams.

Once a transfer is "Here we go!", it becomes official and immediately takes effect.

Players may only represent one team at a time.

All transfers will be published live in the Transfer Centre and permanently recorded in Transfer History.

🎥 VAR

  • Each team starts with 2 VAR challenges.
  • Once used, they are gone permanently.
  • VAR usage is tracked live.
  • Once activated, the acitvity/action that has been completed is immediately disregarded and retaken.

⚡ Team Abilities

  • Every team has one special ability.
  • Each ability can only be used once.
  • Ability usage is tracked live.

  • Bellingham Ab Blast: Thundercunt the ball with an overhead kick top bins leaving the keeper's pants round their ankles (next opposition activity/event is blindfolded).

  • Vorsprung durch Technik: German efficiency, allows a second player to compete against your oposition creating a 2 vs 1.

  • Argentinian Falklands Chant: Enzo may steal 1 beer point from any other team and take it for themselves (cannot be used in final).

  • Brazilian Flair: (As a bonus game), Neymar picks a game of their choice to be played, points to be added to leageue table.
