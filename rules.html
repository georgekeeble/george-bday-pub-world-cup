📜 George's Birthday Pub World Cup Rules

🏆 Objective

The team with the highest total points at the end of the tournament wins the World Cup.

⚽ Match Points

  • Win = 3 Points
  • Draw = 1 Point
  • Loss = 0 Points

🍺 Beer Bonus Points

A team receives 1 bonus league point every time all team members complete a full round of drinks.

🎥 VAR

  • Each team starts with 2 VAR challenges.
  • Once used, they are gone permanently.
  • VAR usage is tracked live.

⚡ Team Abilities

  • Every team has one special ability.
  • Each ability can only be used once.
  • Ability usage is tracked live.
